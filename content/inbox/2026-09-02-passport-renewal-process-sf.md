# Indian passport renewal for kids — San Francisco jurisdiction

Shared process notes for renewing Indian passports for both kids under the
**Consulate General of India, San Francisco (CGI SF)** jurisdiction, which
covers Northern & Central California (including the Bay Area), Colorado,
Hawaii, Utah, Wyoming, and Guam. Child-specific checklists are separate
pages: [Child, age 9](2026-09-02-passport-renewal-child-age-9/) and
[Child, age 16](2026-09-02-passport-renewal-child-age-16/).

## Decisions locked in for both kids

- **Consent form**: **Annexure D** — both parents sign. (Notarization is
  only explicitly required by this checklist if a parent is abroad and
  signing remotely — see "Notarization," below — but notarizing it
  anyway costs little and covers both readings.)
- **Applicant's own declaration**: **Annexure E** — plain-paper
  declaration, signed by the child.
- **Affidavit for Change in Appearance & Signature**: mandatory for
  every applicant regardless of whether appearance actually changed —
  signed by the child.
- **Proof of address**: a parent's **California driving license** — a
  plain color copy, **not notarized**.
- **Proof of the child's US immigration/visa status**: **I-797A approval
  notice** (notarized color copy) — one of several accepted options on
  the checklist; no I-94 is required.
- **Proof of the parents' own US legal status**: each parent's own
  **I-797A approval notice** (notarized color copy) — separate from the
  kids' status documents above.
- **Birth certificate**: each child's **Indian birth certificate** —
  plain copy.
- **Submission method**: **mail-in** for both applications — no VFS
  appointment needed.

## Who does what — and the two portals

- **CGI San Francisco** — sets policy/jurisdiction; doesn't take
  applications directly. Consulate address (reference only, not for
  mailing applications): 71 Stevenson St, Suite 2200, San Francisco, CA
  94105.
- **`mportal.passportindia.gov.in/mission/`** — the actual **government**
  application portal for missions abroad. This is where you register,
  fill in the reissue form, select jurisdiction, upload the photo and
  signature, and get an **ARN** (Application Reference Number). This is
  *not* the same as the VFS site, and it's also not the domestic
  `passportindia.gov.in` portal used inside India.
- **VFS Global** — the outsourced center that actually receives the
  mailed package on CGI SF's behalf. You separately create a profile on
  VFS's own site to arrange payment confirmation and courier/return
  labels; the ARN from the government portal must match the VFS payment
  receipt. Mailing address:
  **VFS Global – San Francisco Center, 642 Harrison Street, Suite 200,
  San Francisco, CA 94107.**
  Helpline: **+1 800 320 9625** (Mon–Fri, 8:00–20:00 PT).

## Process (applies to both kids)

1. **Fill the application** at `https://mportal.passportindia.gov.in/mission/`
   for each child separately — select the correct jurisdiction, choose
   **"Re-issue"**, select **"36 pages"** as the booklet type, and enter
   both an Indian address and a US address. Upload the photo and
   signature here — the application is returned unprocessed if these
   aren't uploaded before applying.
2. **Sign the printed application**: digital signature during the online
   step, plus a physical signature on the last page — both parents also
   sign the last page, below the child's own signature/mark.
3. **Register on the VFS site** to send your documents: create a
   profile, note the ARN, and confirm payment.
4. **Pay the fee** (see below) — card online is simplest; if paying by
   money order or banker's check, it must be made out to **"VFS Services
   (USA) Inc."** — personal checks are not accepted.
5. **Prepare the courier/return label**: either enclose your own prepaid
   return shipping label (its return address must match what's on the
   application) or use VFS's own offered pre-paid label.
6. **Assemble and mail the package** — see each child's own final
   packing checklist for the exact 12-item list.
7. **Track status** online; VFS/CGI SF may flag missing documents, in
   which case you generally have a matter of days to respond before the
   application is put on hold.
8. **Receive the new passport** by mail. Normal processing time quoted
   by CGI SF is **about 3 weeks** from a complete submission (add
   mailing time both directions).

## The 12 mandatory documents (both kids)

Numbered and categorized exactly as they appear on the VFS minor
checklist. Each child's own page turns this into a per-child packing
list.

| # | Document | Category |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application form (filled at `mportal.passportindia.gov.in/mission`, printed, signed) | **Original** |
| 2 | Photograph (2, to spec below — one goes on the application, one on the appearance affidavit) | **Original** (physical prints) |
| 3 | Current/expiring Indian passport | **Original + copy** of first/last (bio & family) pages |
| 4 | Affidavit for Change in Appearance & Signature — signed by the child | **Original** |
| 5 | Annexure E — child's own declaration, plain paper | **Original** |
| 6 | Annexure D — both parents' consent | **Original** |
| 7 | Child's valid US status — I-797A approval notice (or Green Card / visa / EAD / work-study permit — pick whichever applies) | **Notarized color copy** |
| 8 | Proof of address — parent's California driving license | **Color copy** (not notarized) |
| 9 | Parents' Indian passport copies (first/last pages, both parents) | **Self-attested copy** |
| 10 | Parents' own US legal status — each parent's I-797A approval notice | **Notarized color copy** |
| 11 | Child's Indian birth certificate | **Copy** |
| 12 | Fee payment confirmation + return courier label | **Original** (the receipt and the label itself) |

Four different categories, not two — don't notarize things that only
need a plain color copy (item 8) or a self-attested copy (item 9); it
won't hurt, but it's unnecessary notary time and cost.

### Photo spec (corrected)

- **35 mm × 45 mm**, white background — this is *not* the 2"×2" square
  spec used elsewhere; the two photos must be identical.
- Taken within the last 6 months, shoulders visible, no glasses, no
  flash glare, a colored shirt (to stand out against the white
  background), good even lighting on the face, ICAO-compliant.
- One photo affixed to the government application form; the second
  affixed to the Affidavit for Change in Appearance & Signature.

### Notarization — corrected

Only **items 7 and 10** (the child's and parents' US status documents)
are explicitly marked as needing a **notarized** color copy. Proof of
address (8) is a plain color copy, parents' passport copies (9) are
self-attested (the parent signs "true copy" themselves — no notary
needed), and the birth certificate (11) is just a copy. Items 1, 2, 4,
5, 6, and 12 are originals you create/sign yourself; the checklist only
calls out notarization for Annexure D specifically in the case where a
parent is abroad and can't sign in person (their copy must then be
notarized in that country, plus a notarized No Objection Letter) — not
applicable here since both parents are signing directly. The current
passport itself is always the original booklet, never a copy.

## Fees (as researched — confirm current amount on the VFS/government portal before paying)

Both kids get the 36-page booklet, but the government fee differs by
validity choice, since a 10-year passport is billed at the adult rate
even for a minor.

| Item | Approx. cost |
|---|---|
| Minor passport, 5-year/until-18 validity (age 9 child) | ~US$105 |
| Minor passport, full 10-year validity (age 16 child) | ~US$167 |
| VFS service charge | ~US$19 |
| Indian Community Welfare Fund (ICWF) contribution | ~US$2 |
| **Estimated total — age 9 child** | **~US$126** |
| **Estimated total — age 16 child** | **~US$188** |
| Return courier/postage | Separate, if not using VFS's own pre-paid label |
