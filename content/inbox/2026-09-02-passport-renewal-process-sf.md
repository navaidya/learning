# Indian passport renewal for kids — San Francisco jurisdiction

Shared process notes for renewing Indian passports for both kids under the
**Consulate General of India, San Francisco (CGI SF)** jurisdiction, which
covers Northern & Central California (including the Bay Area), Colorado,
Hawaii, Utah, Wyoming, and Guam. Child-specific checklists are separate
pages: [Child, age 9](2026-09-02-passport-renewal-child-age-9/) and
[Child, age 16](2026-09-02-passport-renewal-child-age-16/).

> **Verify before you apply.** This was compiled from `cgisf.gov.in` and
> VFS Global public pages via web search on 2026-09-02 — this session
> could not fetch `cgisf.gov.in` directly (network egress to that domain
> was blocked), so details came from search-result summaries and
> secondhand write-ups, not a direct read of the source pages. Fees,
> addresses, and document lists on Indian government/VFS sites change
> without notice, and small errors here (wrong annexure, expired fee
> figure) can bounce an application. Re-check the primary sources below
> immediately before submitting.

## Who does what

- **CGI San Francisco** — the consulate itself does not accept passport
  applications directly. It sets policy/jurisdiction and handles anything
  VFS can't (e.g. emergency certificates). Consulate address (for
  reference only, not for mailing passport applications): 71 Stevenson
  St, Suite 2200, San Francisco, CA 94105.
- **VFS Global** — the outsourced application/collection center for CGI
  SF. Applications and original documents are mailed to (or dropped off
  at, if attending in person):
  **VFS Global – San Francisco Center, 642 Harrison Street, Suite 200,
  San Francisco, CA 94107.**
  Helpline: **+1 800 320 9625** (Mon–Fri, 8:00–20:00 PT).

## Process (applies to both kids)

1. **Register / log in** on the VFS Global India portal for the USA
   (linked from `cgisf.gov.in` → Passport Related Services →
   `https://visa.vfsglobal.com/usa/en/ind`). This is the online form +
   document upload + appointment/mail-in system for NRI applicants — not
   the domestic `passportindia.gov.in` portal used inside India.
2. **Fill the reissue/renewal application** online for each child
   separately (each kid needs their own application, own fee, own
   document set).
3. **Upload the required documents** to the portal — uploads are
   mandatory before the application can be submitted/mailed.
4. **Pay the fee** online (see below).
5. **Choose mail-in or in-person**: standard renewals in the USA can be
   done by mail — no in-person appointment is required unless you prefer
   one. If booking an in-person slot, VFS allows rescheduling up to twice
   (at least 48 hours before the appointment).
6. **Send the package** (printed application + originals of every
   supporting document + notarized copies where applicable) to the VFS SF
   center address above.
7. **Track status** on the VFS portal. VFS returns incomplete
   applications after ~20 days; if they request more documents, you
   generally have ~7 days to respond.
8. **Receive the new passport** by mail. Normal processing time quoted by
   CGI SF is **about 3 weeks** from a complete submission (add mailing
   time both directions).

## Documents common to both kids

- [ ] Current/expiring Indian passport (original) for the child, plus a
      self-attested photocopy of the first and last pages.
- [ ] **Annexure D** — notarized parental consent, signed by **both**
      parents/legal guardians. Required for every minor application.
- [ ] **Annexure C** instead of D — only if one parent cannot/will not
      give consent (e.g. sole custody, absent parent). Confirm with CGI
      SF/VFS which applies to your situation before applying.
- [ ] Proof of address (self-attested copy): driving license, utility
      bill, telephone bill, lease agreement, or mortgage deed in a
      parent's name. Ask VFS about alternatives if none of these are
      available.
- [ ] Proof of the child's valid US visa/immigration status (e.g. I-20,
      visa page, or equivalent), since this is being filed from the USA.
- [ ] Two recent photographs per photo spec below (even though the
      portal also captures a digital photo).
- [ ] Parents' passports (copies) as identity/relationship proof.

### Photo spec

- 2 in × 2 in (or 35 mm × 35 mm), plain light background, taken within
  the last ~6 months.
- One photo pasted in the box on page 1 of the paper application; the
  second stapled to the blank portion on page 4 ("for official use
  only").

### Notarization rule of thumb

- Applying **by mail**: photocopies of supporting documents (including
  the photo) must be notarized.
- Applying **in person** with originals: no notarization needed for the
  originals themselves — but Annexure D/C is notarized regardless.
